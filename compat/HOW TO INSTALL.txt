LEGO Star Wars: The Complete Saga Portable - installing on your PS Vita
==================================================

You need a homebrew-enabled Vita (HENkaku/Enso) with VitaShell.

ONE-TIME SETUP (skip anything you already have)
  1. kubridge v0.3.1 or newer: put kubridge.skprx in ur0:tai and add this line
     under *KERNEL in ur0:tai/config.txt:
         ur0:tai/kubridge.skprx
  2. fd_fix: put fd_fix.skprx in ur0:tai and add under *KERNEL:
         ur0:tai/fd_fix.skprx
     (Skip fd_fix if you use the rePatch plugin.)
  3. libshacccg.suprx in ur0:data - use ShaRKBR33D to get it in one step.
  4. Reboot the Vita.

INSTALLING THE GAME
  1. Connect the Vita to your PC with VitaShell (USB or FTP).
  2. Copy the "ux0" folder from here onto the Vita's ux0: drive.
     The four .dat files must end up in  ux0:data/lswtcs/
  3. Copy "LEGO Star Wars - The Complete Saga Portable.vpk" anywhere on ux0:, select it in
     VitaShell and press X to install.
  4. Launch "LEGO Star Wars: The Complete Saga Portable" from the home screen.

If the game says files are missing, check that ux0:data/lswtcs/ contains
Audio.dat, Levels.dat, Others.dat and Textures.dat.
